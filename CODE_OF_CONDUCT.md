# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SOCGenie is a learning-oriented cybersecurity project. We want everyone who takes
part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, to be able to do so in a
respectful, harassment-free and constructive environment, regardless of experience
level, background, identity or opinion.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; explain the reasoning behind a
  review comment.
- Be welcoming to newcomers and to people with less security or ML experience.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical where possible.
- Credit other people's work and cite sources.
- Be honest about what the software does and does not do, including its limits.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Sustained pressure, or demands on a maintainer's time beyond reasonable
  expectations of a student project.
- Using this project or its issue tracker to target, probe or attack systems or
  people. SOCGenie is a defensive project; offensive or unauthorised activity has
  no place here.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Responsible security discussion

Discussing vulnerabilities is welcome, but do it responsibly: do not post working
exploits or sensitive details publicly, and report potential vulnerabilities
privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews, and to individuals representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
project maintainer, [@SargunKaur15](https://github.com/SargunKaur15), through their
GitHub profile or a private message. Please do not report conduct concerns in a
public issue. Include what happened, where, and any links or screenshots that help.

Reports are treated with discretion. The maintainer may respond by asking for a
change in behaviour, removing or editing comments, locking a thread, or restricting
or blocking participation, depending on severity.

## Attribution
